    Is this a scam?+

    Fair question, and a good habit. This page asks for nothing except a website name, and it only reads public settings that every email service can see. You can confirm the result with Google's free tool at toolbox.googleapps.com/apps/checkmx. If you ask us to fix it, you pay only after the result turns green.

    My IT person or web host handles our email. Isn't this already done?+

    Often not. Setting up email and protecting it from fakes are different jobs. Of 26 Dubai trading and logistics companies we checked in November 2026, 23 could be faked. The check takes 20 seconds, so it's worth knowing for sure.

    Will fixing it stop my own emails?+

    Not when it's done in stages. We first watch who sends email for you, make sure every real system is on the approved list, and only then switch on blocking.

    How long does it take?+

    The setup is one session with you. Then about two weeks of watching, and a week at the “spam folder” stage, before fakes are fully refused.

    What is a copycat domain?+

    A website address one letter away from yours, like the letter o swapped for a zero. Fraudsters register them to send convincing emails. Being registered doesn't make one dangerous, but it's worth watching.

    What do DMARC, SPF and DKIM mean?+

    They're the technical names. SPF is your approved senders list, DKIM is the digital signature on your emails, and DMARC is the instruction that tells Gmail and Outlook to block fakes. Only DMARC set to “quarantine” or “reject” actually stops them.
