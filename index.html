FAQ

Straight answers

What is DMARC?

A text record in your domain's DNS that tells mail servers (Gmail, Outlook and others) what to do with email that claims to be from you but fails the SPF and DKIM checks: nothing (none), send it to spam (quarantine), or refuse it (reject).

Does p=none protect me?

No. p=none is watch-only: you receive reports, but fake emails are delivered as normal. Protection starts at quarantine and is complete at reject.

What is a lookalike domain?

A domain one letter away from yours, or the same name on another ending, like the letter o swapped for a zero. Fraudsters use them to send convincing emails even when your own domain is protected. Being registered doesn't make one malicious, but it's worth watching.

Is this check safe? What do you store?

It reads public DNS records only, through Google Public DNS, from inside your browser. It doesn't send email, doesn't log in anywhere, and we don't store the domain you type.

Will the fix stop my real emails?

Not when it's done in stages. We start in watch mode and list everyone who sends as you, then move to quarantine, then reject. Jumping straight to reject is what causes problems.

How long does it take?

The setup itself is one session with you. Then one to two weeks of watching, and a week at quarantine, before full reject.
